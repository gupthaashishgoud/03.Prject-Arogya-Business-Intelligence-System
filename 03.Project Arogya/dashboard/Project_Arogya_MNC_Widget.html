Hourly SMS/WhatsApp to AttendantEvery 60 min during surgery - status update with stage, vitals, ETA
2
On-Demand Status QueryAttendant replies "STATUS" - instant automated response with live OT status
3
Delay Alert Auto-PushIf surgery exceeds ETA by >30 min - auto-alert to attendant + reason
4
Completion ConfirmationSurgeon closes - instant "Surgery completed" + recovery room ETA
Why Patient Journey Intelligence Matters:

Surgery waiting is the most stressful time for families. Hourly updates reduce anxiety, reduce front-desk queries by 70%, and build trust. When attendants feel informed, they become advocates - referring friends and family. This is not just automation; it is intelligence that transforms anxiety into advocacy.
